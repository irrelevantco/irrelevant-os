# Sales workflow

## Current manual workflow

1. Sign in and choose the correct organization. Add a public business record and its source. Unknown fields stay empty; contract value is an explicit operator estimate.
2. Discovered → Researching: inspect permitted public sources. Record dated notes with links and distinguish evidence from assumptions.
3. Qualified: select businesses that could realistically benefit from the company's services. Phase 1 does not compute qualification scores.
4. Ready for Outreach: research and context are ready. This label does not approve an email. Phase 1 has no send capability.
5. Contacted → Replied → Meeting Scheduled: record actual external progress manually in notes and follow-up dates. Future Gmail/Calendar integrations will reconcile real events.
6. Audit Delivered → Proposal Sent: only record real delivery. Future artifacts need human approval and explicit external delivery authorization.
7. Won: record the business decision manually. Until contract/payment modules exist, do not interpret this as evidence of a signed agreement or collected cash.
8. Lost: record the reason in a note. Suppressed: record an opt-out/no-contact requirement. There is no outreach in Phase 1; Phase 4 must add a dedicated suppression ledger enforced independently of pipeline status.

Use the follow-up queue each workday. It includes today and overdue leads and excludes Won/Lost/Suppressed. Prioritize a manageable number of suitable companies; a high lead count is not success. Use notes for call outcomes and next steps; dedicated call logs arrive later.

## Future approval boundaries

A Growth Review needs approval of its exact version before external delivery. An email draft requires a separate exact-content approval, sender identity/postal configuration, suppression checks and bounded send limits. Approval must expire on edits. Payment schedule changes and publishing require explicit authorization. A signed annual agreement must exist separately from Stripe recurring billing.

## Future client handoff

When the agreement is signed, create a workspace with defined scope, deliverables, revision limits, timelines and payment schedule. Track direct project costs and calculate gross margin using actual recognized revenue/cost definitions. Quarterly installments must not be mislabeled monthly recurring revenue. Add creator assignments later without changing the contract of existing client workspaces.

Do not invent case-study results or rely on unverified creator revenue claims when writing outreach or sales material.
